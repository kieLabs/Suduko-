How to play Sudoku

Fill the 9 × 9 grid so every row, column and 3 × 3 box contains digits 1 through 9 exactly once.

1. Select a squareTap a cell
2. Enter a digit1 to 9
3. Use notesCandidate mode
4. Need help?Hint
5. Check your gridCheck
6. Finish the puzzleOne solution

A number is not allowed to repeat in any row, column or 3 × 3 box. Given numbers are fixed. Pencil notes are possible candidates, not confirmed answers.

SudokuVerse

Infinite Puzzle Challenge · GamerKie Edition

SudokuVerse is an original browser Sudoku game built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Puzzles are generated on your device and your progress is stored locally in this browser.

Your saves do not automatically sync between devices. The local records page is not a real global leaderboard. Some advanced difficulty labels represent challenge tiers; this version validates unique solutions but does not claim to implement every named expert solving technique such as forcing chains or Swordfish.
